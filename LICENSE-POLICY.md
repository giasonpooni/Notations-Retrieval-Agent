## Proposed future boundary

The proposed future production continuation is operational control-plane software:
typed execution-plan eligibility, authorization and scientific dispatch policy.
The proposal is to develop future proprietary first-party additions under reviewed
terms in an explicitly authorized private continuation. That continuation has not
been created, renamed, published or made private by this draft.

Any future public generic reference or interface subset requires a separately
specified scope. No source extraction, architectural split, dependency update,
scientific change, or new copyright ownership claim is included here.

## Why this draft does not replace the root license

Adoption of proprietary terms is blocked on confirmation of rights-holder
authority and review of the actual proprietary terms. Visible Git authorship and a contributors copyright notice
do not establish that all contributions have been assigned to one entity or identify
the party entitled to grant a new proprietary license. No assignment, CLA or counsel-
reviewed proprietary terms were supplied for this transition.

Consequently this is a policy-only draft: it does not invent a restrictive license,
assert retroactive exclusivity, or change `pyproject.toml` from `license = "MIT"` to a
proprietary identifier without supporting terms. Before a future release, identify
which new files or additions are owned and licensable under the approved proprietary
terms, which legacy MIT portions remain, and how notices are preserved. Resolve any
remaining contributor/ownership questions and approve the precise release boundary.
